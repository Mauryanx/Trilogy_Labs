How We Use Data

  1. Provide core functions (auth — including anonymous mode, preference storage, AI image generation).
  2. Schedule and deliver daily inspiration via push notifications.
  3. Improve stability with diagnostics and Crashlytics error reports.
  4. Prevent fraud/abuse (e.g., token hygiene, rate limits).
  5. Send essential service messages (e.g., account, delivery, or error notices).

We do not show third-party ads, do not use advertising identifiers, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Image Generation:

  • Prompts/themes are sent via secure API to an AI image generation service (OpenAI Image API) to create inspirational quote images.
  • Processing occurs through secure Firebase Cloud Functions acting as an intermediary.
  • The AI provider processes requests transiently and does not use your content for advertising.

Data Minimization:

  • We only send the minimum necessary prompt/theme data to generate your images.
  • No personal contact lists, precise location, or ad identifiers are used.
  • Generated images are saved to your account only so you can access or delete them.

Data may be processed in the United States or other regions where our providers operate. Transfers from the EEA/UK/Switzerland rely on EU Standard Contractual Clauses and Data-Privacy-Framework self-certifications where applicable.

Your Rights

You can access, rectify, export, or delete your data and withdraw consent at any time via in-app controls or email team@trilogy-labs.com. EEA/UK users may also lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

California / U.S. State Disclosures

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

Collected categories: identifiers (UID, FCM token), contact info (email if provided), user content (generated images and prompts), internet activity (app interactions), diagnostics (crash/error logs).

Right to know, delete, or correct: use in-app controls or email (same as above).

Security

Encryption: All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (e.g., Firebase AES-256). Access Controls: Firebase Authentication + security rules limit access to the owner’s data. Processing Limitation: Data is used solely to generate, deliver, and store inspirational images and notifications. Minimal Data: We collect only what’s necessary to run the service reliably.

Data Retention & Deletion

Account and preference data are kept while your account exists. Generated images remain until you delete them. Diagnostic logs are typically retained 30–90 days. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal data from active systems, with limited backup copies retained for up to 180 days.

Automated Processing

AI image creation is automated and used only to generate your inspirational content. It does not produce legal or similarly significant effects.
