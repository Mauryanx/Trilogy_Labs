How We Use Data

  1. Identify plants using GPT-4o Vision AI.
  2. Show relevant ads through AdMob (with personalization options).
  3. Improve stability and performance through analytics and crash logs.
  4. Deliver notifications (if enabled).

Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI: Used for plant image recognition (images not stored permanently).
  • Firebase: Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Storage.
  • Google AdMob: Advertising and performance metrics.

Data Security

We apply security measures including encrypted transmission, authentication controls, and minimal data storage. Photos are deleted when you request or remove them in-app.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to your data
  • Delete photos or your account at any time
  • Opt-out of analytics (device settings)
  • Control ad personalization via system settings

International Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States or other regions where Firebase and OpenAI operate. Transfers rely on EU Standard Contractual Clauses and provider safeguards.

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us to request deletion.

Changes
