Age Requirement

Refina is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. By creating an account or using the app you confirm that you are at least 18. The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, email team@trilogy-labs.com and we will delete it.

Medical Disclaimer

Refina is a cosmetic visualization and educational tool. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a medical device, and using it does not create a doctor-patient relationship.

  • AI previews are illustrative renderings, not predictions. They are not a simulation of surgery and do not forecast the result of any procedure.
  • The Facial Dossier is a descriptive, automated analysis of your photographs. Its observations, measurements and comparisons are generated by AI, are approximate, and may be inaccurate. It is not a clinical assessment and must not be used to diagnose or treat any condition.
  • The AI Advisor and dossier chat are automated assistants, not clinicians. They can make mistakes and can produce incorrect information.
  • Results are not guaranteed. Nothing in Refina is a licensed medical opinion, and Refina is not operated or supervised by physicians.

Always consult a qualified, licensed professional before making any decision about a cosmetic procedure, medication or treatment. If you have a medical concern, seek care from a licensed provider; in an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

How We Use Data

  1. Provide core functions (login, photo upload, AI previews and Facial Dossiers).
  2. Validate trials and subscriptions via Superwall.
  3. Prevent trial abuse or fraud (device & IP checks).
  4. Improve stability through crash and performance reports.
  5. Send essential emails or in-app messages (e.g., password reset, receipt).

We do not show third-party ads inside Refina, and we never sell your personal data. We do measure how our own ads perform: the Meta (Facebook) SDK shares your device advertising identifier and basic app events, such as install and subscription, with Meta Platforms, Inc. On iOS this happens only if you allow tracking when the system prompt appears. On Android you can limit it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads, by deleting or resetting your advertising ID. We never send your photos, your Facial Dossier, or your advisor conversations to Meta or to any advertising partner.

Facial Data Protection

  • Encryption: All facial images encrypted in transit
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication required
  • Processing Limitation: Used only to generate the AI previews, motion clips and Facial Dossier reports you request
  • No Identification: We do not use facial recognition to identify you, and we do not match your photos against any database of identified people or share them for identification purposes
  • Secure Processing: AI processing via Firebase Cloud Functions
  • User Control: Users can delete their facial data anytime
  • Data Minimization: Only required photos are collected

AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Facial Enhancement & Dossier Processing:

  • When a user requests an AI preview, a motion clip or a Facial Dossier, their facial photos, user-provided profile details (such as age, gender, and ethnicity), and selected parameters are securely transmitted via API to our third-party AI processing providers: Replicate, Inc., OpenAI, L.L.C., Anthropic, PBC, OpenRouter, Inc., and Features & Labels, Inc. (fal.ai). OpenRouter routes requests to the model providers that serve them, currently Google LLC (Gemini), Alibaba Cloud (Qwen) and Moonshot AI (Kimi, served through specialist inference hosts). fal.ai routes motion-clip generation to MiniMax.
  • Not every provider receives every request. Which providers are involved depends on the feature you use and on availability at the time; the list above is the complete set that may receive facial images or profile details.
  • This transmission occurs only after the user voluntarily uploads photos and requests AI processing. By initiating generation, the user consents to this secure transmission.
  • The transmitted data may include the uploaded facial photos, user-provided profile details, and the selected cosmetic enhancement or analysis parameters required to generate the result.
  • Processing occurs through our secure backend acting as an intermediary.
  • We use these providers under API terms that do not permit them to train their models on data submitted through the API, and we enable no-retention options where a provider offers them. We rely on each provider's contractual commitments; we cannot independently audit their internal practices.
  • The resulting previews, clips and reports are returned to the app and stored in the user's account storage.

Data Minimization:

  • Only the facial photos necessary for enhancement previews and Facial Dossier reports are processed.
  • No facial data is used for advertising, marketing, or research purposes.
  • No facial data is shared with advertising networks or social media platforms.

International Transfers

Your Rights

You may access, export, or delete your data at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete Account. If you no longer have the app installed, use our account deletion request page, or email team@trilogy-labs.com.

Security

All images encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Strict authentication ensures users only access their own data.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your photos, generated previews, motion clips and Facial Dossier reports are kept in your account until you delete them or delete your account. They are not deleted on a timer, because the app is designed to let you reopen your results later and across devices. You can delete individual photos and results at any time in the app, and deleting your account removes all of it.

When you delete something it is removed from active storage immediately. Our cloud storage provider keeps deleted objects in a recoverable state for a short window (currently 7 days) before they are permanently destroyed, and encrypted backups are purged within 180 days. Copies sent to AI providers for processing are governed by those providers' own retention terms, listed in the sub-processor table above.

Account data is kept while the account exists. Purchase records are retained for up to 10 years where tax and accounting law requires it, separately from your profile.

Automated Processing

AI preview and Facial Dossier generation is automated but has no legal or similarly significant effect.
