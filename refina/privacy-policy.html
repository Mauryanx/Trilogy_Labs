How We Use Data

  1. Provide core functions (login, photo upload, AI previews and Facial Dossiers).
  2. Validate trials and subscriptions via Superwall.
  3. Prevent trial abuse or fraud (device & IP checks).
  4. Improve stability through crash and performance reports.
  5. Send essential emails or in-app messages (e.g., password reset, receipt).

We do not show third-party ads inside Refina, and we never sell your personal data. We do measure how our own ads perform: the Meta (Facebook) SDK shares your device advertising identifier and basic app events, such as install and subscription, with Meta Platforms, Inc. On iOS this happens only if you allow tracking when the system prompt appears. On Android you can limit it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads, by deleting or resetting your advertising ID. We never send your photos, your Facial Dossier, or your advisor conversations to Meta or to any advertising partner.

AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Facial Enhancement & Dossier Processing:

  • When a user requests an AI preview or a Facial Dossier, their facial photos, user-provided profile details (such as age, gender, and ethnicity), and selected parameters are securely transmitted via API to our third-party AI processing providers: Replicate, Inc., OpenAI, L.L.C., and OpenRouter, Inc. OpenRouter routes requests to the model providers that serve them, currently Google LLC (Gemini) and Alibaba Cloud (Qwen).
  • This transmission occurs only after the user voluntarily uploads photos and requests AI processing. By initiating generation, the user consents to this secure transmission.
  • The transmitted data may include the uploaded facial photos, user-provided profile details, and the selected cosmetic enhancement or analysis parameters required to generate the result.
  • Processing occurs through our secure backend acting as an intermediary.
  • These providers process the data solely to generate the requested results and do not use it to train their models.
  • The resulting previews and reports are returned to the app and stored only in the user's secure account storage.

Data Minimization:

  • Only the facial photos necessary for enhancement previews and Facial Dossier reports are processed.
  • No facial data is used for advertising, marketing, or research purposes.
  • No facial data is shared with advertising networks or social media platforms.
